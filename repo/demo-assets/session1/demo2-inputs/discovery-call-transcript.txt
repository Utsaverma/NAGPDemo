Discovery call - Bulk Invoice Approval feature
Attendees: Priya (Finance Ops Lead), Sam (Product), Meera (Engineering)

Priya: Right now our approvers open every invoice one at a time. For a
school district with 40 invoices in a batch, that's 40 clicks. We want
to select several and approve them together.

Sam: Got it. Would you want a summary before they confirm, or just
approve immediately?

Priya: Definitely a summary first - total amount, number of invoices,
which customers. Then confirm.

Meera: Can someone override one invoice in the batch, like exclude it
or approve a different amount?

Priya: Yes, we need a per-item override. Sometimes one invoice in the
batch needs a partial approval, not full.

Sam: What's the approval threshold where we'd want a second approver?
Priya: Anything over $500 should need two people to sign off.

Meera: Do we need an audit trail?
Priya: Yes - who approved what, and when, for every item.
